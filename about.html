About Kemeya Azezi

I'm Kemeya Azezi. I'm currently based in London, but Brussels holds a lasting place in my life — it's where a lot of who I am took shape, and it stays woven into how I think about tea and about this project.

This website exists for one simple reason: I love tea. Not as a lifestyle brand or a business, but as a genuine, ongoing curiosity — how it's grown on six continents, how the same leaf becomes green, black, oolong, white or pu-erh depending on how it's handled, how different cultures turned it into ritual, and what the actual science says once you strip away the wellness-industry noise. Coffee earns a place here too, as tea's closest relative in the world of ritual drinks.

What this site is

  • A growing, honest reference on the origins of tea and coffee, region by region.
  • A place for the people who shaped tea culture — historical and contemporary specialists.
  • A plain-language look at the science behind tea, without the exaggerated health claims.
  • A blog where I note down what I'm learning, tasting, and thinking about.

What this site is not

It is not a shop. I don't sell tea, courses, or consulting here, and I don't run ads or affiliate links. If a page ever recommends a brand or product, it's because I genuinely think it's useful — not because I was paid to say so.

Find me here

kemeyaazezi.com is the place to find what I actually write and think. I'm slowly building out a presence on other platforms as well, but this site is the source.
