Privacy

This site is a small, non-commercial project. It doesn't use cookies, analytics, or any kind of tracking. The only personal information it handles is what you choose to send through the contact form, and this page explains what happens to it.

What's collected

The contact form asks for your name, your email address, and your message. That's all. Nothing is collected automatically, and nothing is collected anywhere else on the site.

Why

This information is used only to read your message and reply to you. It isn't used for marketing, added to a mailing list, or shared for any other purpose.

Who processes it

Submissions are delivered by Formspree, a third-party form service, which forwards them to a private inbox. Formspree processes this data on the site's behalf under its own privacy policy, linked above. No other third party receives it, and it is never sold.

How long it's kept

Messages are kept only as long as needed to respond to them, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask what information was submitted about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, at any time, by using the contact form itself.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies and does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.
