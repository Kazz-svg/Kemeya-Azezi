Why this site exists

2 September 2026

I wanted a place to put down everything I find interesting about tea — properly, not as a scattered set of notes app entries or half-finished thoughts. kemeyaazezi.com is that place.

A few things I wanted to be true about it from the start:

  • No shop. I'm not selling tea, courses, or "the secret to the perfect cup." If I ever mention a brand, it's because I actually think it's good.
  • No filler. Every page here is meant to say something real — the goal is a small, dense, honestly-written reference, not a pile of thin pages built to rank for keywords.
  • Global, but personal. I wanted a proper world tour of tea — continent by continent — but also a site that's unmistakably mine: written from London, with Brussels, where I'm from in every way that matters to me, running through it.

This is a side project, so it'll grow slowly and unevenly — a continent page one week, a note on tea chemistry the next, maybe eventually more coffee content. If you're reading this early, thank you for finding it while it's still small.
